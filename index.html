About PT Maxim Cipta Indo

PT Maxim Cipta Indo is a multi-vertical company focused on hospitality and lifestyle businesses across Indonesia. Our pillars: hospitality excellence, neighborhood cafés, productive coworking environments, and social billiard spaces. We prioritize safety, hospitality, and local community engagement.

  • Headquarters: Medan, Indonesia
  • Focus: Hotel operations, café concepts, coworking management, billiard lounges
  • Vision: To be the trusted operator for local hospitality and leisure experiences
